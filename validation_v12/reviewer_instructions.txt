INSTRUCTIONS FOR REVIEWERS
Read each project's title and abstract on the Review tab. Answer from the text only. Do not look up the grant elsewhere.
For each row, fill in: WHR (yes/no), CV (yes/no), CV-WHR (yes/no), a WHR category if WHR = yes (or "Not WHR"), and any comments. Use Comments for borderline calls.
Rows are in random order. The pipeline's calls are hidden. Some rows were sampled more heavily than others on purpose, so do not infer anything from the mix.

WOMEN'S HEALTH RESEARCH (WHR): count as yes if any of the rules below applies.
1. Female-specific conditions and reproductive health (e.g., menopause, endometriosis, PCOS, contraception, gynecologic conditions).
2. Pregnancy research counts only if it studies maternal outcomes (the pregnant person's health). Research only on offspring or fetal outcomes does not count.
3. Female cancers count, including breast cancer (and ovarian, uterine, cervical cancers).
4. Female-predominant conditions (conditions that mostly or disproportionately affect women, such as lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, Sjogren's, scleroderma, autoimmune thyroid disease, osteoporosis, migraine, fibromyalgia, interstitial cystitis, urinary incontinence, SCAD, takotsubo):
   - If the condition is named in the TITLE, it counts.
   - If it is named only in the ABSTRACT, it counts only if the project is also women-focused (women named in the title, repeated focus on women in the abstract, or a substantive sex-differences aim).
   - Studies of men only never qualify through this rule.
5. Always count as WHR, regardless of women focus: irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), binge-eating disorder, INOCA (ischemia with no obstructive coronary arteries), MINOCA (MI with non-obstructive coronary arteries), and pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH).
6. Count only with a women or sex-differences focus: HFpEF, Alzheimer's disease/dementia, depression, anxiety, PTSD, and intimate partner violence (IPV).
7. Research that substantively studies sex differences or conditions that affect women differently counts. Merely adjusting for sex, or enrolling both sexes, does not.
8. Other women-focused research (e.g., women's cohorts, HIV/STI prevention in women, sexual violence, HPV vaccination) counts.

CARDIOVASCULAR (CV): yes if the project's research is about the heart or blood vessels (e.g., coronary disease, heart failure, arrhythmia, hypertension, stroke and cerebrovascular disease, vascular biology).
- Any cardiotoxicity counts as CV (e.g., cardiotoxicity from cancer therapy).
- These alone do NOT count as CV: blood disorders (e.g., anemia, sickle cell, clotting factor disorders without vascular outcomes), tumor blood vessels / tumor angiogenesis, and blood-brain-barrier injury or white-matter lesions on their own.

CV-WHR: yes only if the project is both CV = yes and WHR = yes.

WHR CATEGORY: pick the single best fit from the dropdown, or "Not WHR".
